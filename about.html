About Maminor Foods

Nourishing Lives Naturally.

Maminor Foods Nigeria Limited is a food processing and packaging company committed to producing healthy, nutrient-rich foods and drinks from indigenous Nigerian grains.

We are passionate about promoting healthy living, supporting local farmers and providing affordable nutrition for everyone.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, highly nutritious, and minimally processed foods that promote everyday wellness, while empowering local agricultural communities.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of indigenous natural foods in Africa, recognized for quality, health benefits, and sustainable practices.
